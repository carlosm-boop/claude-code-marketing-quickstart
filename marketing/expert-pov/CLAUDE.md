# marketing/expert-pov/ — your founder POV + OBI

This folder holds your **expert / founder point of view**: core beliefs, contrarian takes, origin stories, and the recommended "one big idea" (OBI) that anchors thought leadership and brand voice.

**To populate:** run `/expert-pov` with the founder's content (LinkedIn posts, podcasts, interviews) or their answers to the question bank. Thin input halts before OBI synthesis — the skill asks for more rather than invent a POV.

**For the format:** see [`../../pulse-analytics-example/expert-pov/expert-pov.md`](../../pulse-analytics-example/expert-pov/expert-pov.md) — fully populated example for the fictional PulseAnalytics B2B SaaS analytics platform.
