## One-liner

A global technology consulting and product-engineering company specialized in **legacy modernization and AI-native delivery**, backed by MongoDB.

**Short form:** *From legacy to intelligent. End to end.*

## Worldview (the thought-leadership platform)

Software is moving from static **systems of record** to intelligent **systems of execution**. Access to models is commoditized; **operationalizing intelligence is the hard part**. Enterprises are stuck on fragmented legacy, and the bridge to AI-native operations isn't bought — it's built. That bridge is WeKan.

This is what WeKan publishes, speaks, and campaigns on: *the enterprise that wins is the one that operationalizes intelligence — and that starts with modernization, not models.*

## The arc

Legacy reality → **Transform & modernize (Evolve)** → **Build & engineer (Helix)** → **Operationalize intelligence (NitroStack)**

One partner, one continuous context. AI is the end state, not a bolt-on. WeKan owns the entire arc — not a staff-aug vendor, not a migration shop.
