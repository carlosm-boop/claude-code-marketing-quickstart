# WeKan.AI — brand kit (canonical)

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary accent (coral) | `#FF5A78` | Eyebrows, rules, key words, badges, arrows, logo mark, CTA highlights |
| Ink / headings | `#1A202C` | Big bold headlines, dark CTA band background |
| Mid ink | `#2D3748` | Secondary dark surfaces, strong body |
| Body text | `#4A5568` | Default paragraph/body copy |
| Muted | `#718096` | Eyebrow labels, sub-labels, captions |
| Faint | `#A0AEC0` | Footer text, de-emphasized meta |
| Light surface | `#F1F5F9` | Card fills, subtle panels |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Page background, text on dark/coral |
| Coral wash | `#FFF1F4` | Highlighted / featured card fills |

**Coral is a signature accent, not a fill.** One or two coral moments per slide, section, or fold. Never more than two accent colors in one composition.

## Typography

**Poppins** is the brand typeface. Fallback: Calibri, then a clean sans.

- **Headlines** — Poppins Bold, large, tight leading, `#1A202C`. 32–44pt on a content slide, larger on a cover.
- **Body** — Poppins Regular, `#4A5568`. 12–16pt.
- **Eyebrow labels** — Poppins, UPPERCASE, letter-spaced, 10–11pt, `#FF5A78` (or `#718096` secondary).

## Recurring layout motifs

- **Eyebrow → rule → headline → subhead** stack, with a short coral underline rule (~60px)
- **Cards** — rounded corners (12–16px), thin light border or `#F1F5F9` fill, generous internal padding
- **Numbered badges** — `01 02 03` in coral, small, top-left of cards
- **Pill tags** — small rounded outline pills for labels like `WeKan Evolve`, `NitroStack`, `Helix`
- **Coral arrows** (→) connecting sequential cards to show the journey
- **Dark CTA band** — full-width `#1A202C` bar with white text and a coral accent, for the one-line takeaway
- **Icon chips** — small rounded square, light fill, coral glyph

## Logo & footer

Logo: a **coral filled circle** with a lowercase **"w"** in white, next to the "WeKan" wordmark (Poppins Bold) and the tagline "AI-Native Engineering Partner" in tracked muted caps.

Footer on content slides: small muted "WeKan · From Legacy to Intelligent" left, page number right, thin divider rule above.

## Visual tone

Confident, clean, senior. Strategy material for Fortune 500 CTOs — not a startup pitch. **Avoid:** stock-photo clutter, decorative gradients, emoji, more than two accent colors. Whitespace and one strong coral accent carry each composition.

## Format defaults

- Decks: 16:9, 13.33" × 7.5" (960 × 540 pt), white background
- Web: white ground, near-white `#F7FAFC` for full-bleed bands
