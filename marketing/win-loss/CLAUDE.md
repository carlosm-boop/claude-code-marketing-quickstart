# marketing/win-loss/ — your win/loss analysis

This folder holds your **win/loss analysis**: why deals close and why they don't, in the buyer's own words — patterns across product, messaging, GTM, pricing, competition, and customer context. It's the evidence base under positioning, messaging, and ICP.

**To populate:** run `/win-loss-analysis` against your won + lost sales-call transcripts. Redact PII first — see [`../../.claude/rules/pii-redaction.md`](../../.claude/rules/pii-redaction.md) — and bind every claim to a verbatim quote per [`../../.claude/rules/evidence-bound-outputs.md`](../../.claude/rules/evidence-bound-outputs.md).
